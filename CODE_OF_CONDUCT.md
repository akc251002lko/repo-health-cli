
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive,
and professional environment for everyone participating
in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Communicate professionally.
- Accept reasonable criticism.
- Focus on technical discussions.
- Respect different perspectives.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive or abusive communication.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the
repository maintainers through the GitHub contact
or reporting channels available to you.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces,
including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other project-related interactions.
